WHY MEDVIEW EXISTS

From isolated imaging experiments to a tool someone else can understand and use.

Earlier medical-imaging projects often ended as independent proofs of concept: one dataset, one algorithm, one result. They demonstrated an idea, but the work was not carried forward into a reusable review workflow.

MedView reframes that experience around the person who must inspect the volume, review a segmentation, correct it, measure it, and export a traceable result. The public browser demo helps anyone understand the workflow; the repository contains the full Python/C++ implementation, validation layers, and tests.

Portfolio and education software. No clinical validation, diagnostic claims, patient data, or regulatory approval.
